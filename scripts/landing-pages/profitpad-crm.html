Why it’s free
We don’t think people will pay for software much longer. If we didn’t give this away, someone else would.

With AI, anyone can rebuild the core of their favourite tool in a weekend. We put far more than a weekend into this project, because we wanted a complete, fully working CRM, not a demo. We think paying a monthly fee for software is going to fade fast over the next couple of years.

So we’re giving it away. Our goal is simple: a great CRM that people are happy to use, at no cost.

What still needs a person is the setup. Knowing which reports managers need, what reps should see first, and how marketing and admins should work takes real conversations with your team. That is the part worth paying for, and it’s the only part we charge for.

We’re also open to feedback on the base CRM. Tell us what should change.

What ProfitPad CRM does instead
Reporting needs a sync job and a second database.
Instead
The CRM is the database. Every property is one real, typed Postgres column, and reporting gets its own read-only schema.
Every new field or object is an afternoon of clicking.
Instead
Ask an AI assistant. New objects, properties, calculations and permissions go through rules the database itself enforces.
Linked records are hard to query and easy to duplicate.
Instead
One association table, each link stored once, with labels. Merges and deletes are explicit.
The data lives on someone else’s platform.
Instead

Run it

Your server, your data, in six steps.

Everything runs in Docker from published images. Nothing to clone or build.

1
Set up the server

Any Linux server

With Docker, at least 2 CPU cores, 4 GB of memory and 50 GB of storage.

2
Create the sign-in apps

Google or Microsoft

An OAuth client for signing in, and a service account so the CRM can send email from your team’s mailboxes.

3
Point DNS

One DNS record

Point an address like crm.yourcompany.com at the server, and have your reverse proxy serve it over HTTPS.

4
Install it

One command

The installer asks for your address, sign-in keys and first admin, then starts the CRM on port 8080, leaving 80 and 443 free.

5
Sign in

No passwords

Sign in with Google or Microsoft. Only people with a user record get in, and admins add the rest.

6
Set it up

Make it yours

Add your objects, properties, pipelines and views, by hand or by asking AI. Or hire us to do it with your team.
